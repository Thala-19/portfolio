Visora — live assistant for the visually impaired

An AI live assistant that helps visually impaired users perceive and navigate the world through conversation. With two peers, I created the baseline workflow for voice-to-voice interaction — wiring Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech around a text-understanding model so a person can speak and be answered, same as anyone.

This was my first real exposure to API integration. Learning to build my own model pipeline and automated process flows here became the foundation for most of what I've made since.

What it does

Listens, understands, and speaks back in one continuous flow — an accessibility-first AI companion.
